# Grok Imagine Odyssey (contest + Imagine shorts)

Use this for the 2026 Grok Imagine × Odyssey contest reel. This is **not** FAL H3. Do not mix pipelines.

## Look lock (2026-08-31)

Obscure late-1970s / early-1980s gothic dark-fantasy film universe. Analog print, not digital. Practical effects, rubber creatures, miniatures, theatrical sets. Soft focus, heavy 16mm/35mm grain, bloom, dust, scratches. Underexposed, crushed blacks, murky shadows. Deep cobalt moonlight mixed with warm torch/candle. Faded analog color, low dynamic range, imperfect optical compositing. Eerie, dreamlike, theatrical. Video: vintage handheld, film motion, not hyper-smooth.

Never: clean modern digital, glossy CGI, bright sat, crisp commercial light, ultra-sharp, modern fashion, anime, pixel, AI-slop, sci-fi polish.

**Anime and pixel R2V pipelines are dead.** Ashen discarded those clips as unmatched slop.

## Period / cast

- Odysseus: original face, dark eyes, tight curls, beard, forehead gash. Not a celebrity.
- Warriors: Late Bronze Age Mycenaean bronze cuirass, leather pteruges, greaves, round shields. Not Roman. Not a tunic.
- Ocean: huge war galley, ~30 armored men on deck. Never a one-man skiff. Nobody wading in the water.
- Odysseus suffers. He does not scream orders at the crew.
- Cyclops: one eye in the forehead. Never write “wet glass eye.” Never two eyes.

## Prompt craft

Structure: **subject → action → camera → atmosphere/style → hard constraints**.

- No mini-screenplays. No 0–3s / 3–7s / 7–10s beat sheets. xAI examples are short.
- Action must be the **movie beat**, not idle motion. “Necks slowly lower, oars drag” is a 10s of nothing. Write the event (Scylla grabs crew off the packed galley; men flee the cyclops cave after walking into his house).
- Do not use “straining” (read as screaming at crew). Write suffering, helpless, bound, watching.
- Do not write “bunch of dirty armored men.” Write Mycenaean Greek, Late Bronze Age, bronze cuirass.
- No franchise names in prompts (Godzilla, Toho, Harryhausen).
